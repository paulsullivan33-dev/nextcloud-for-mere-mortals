WebDAV is plain HTTP with extra verbs. The lab drove it
with curl and the admin's own credentials
(D=http://127.0.0.1:8090/remote.php/dav/files/ruth):

$ curl -u ruth:<redacted> -T pancake-recipe-v1.txt $D/pancake-recipe.txt
HTTP 201      (created)

$ curl -u ruth:<redacted> -T book-club-notes.txt $D/book-club-notes.txt
HTTP 201

$ curl -u ruth:<redacted> -X PROPFIND -H "Depth: 1" $D/
(names in the listing, trimmed)
/remote.php/dav/files/ruth/
/remote.php/dav/files/ruth/Nextcloud%20Manual.pdf
/remote.php/dav/files/ruth/Templates/
/remote.php/dav/files/ruth/Reasons%20to%20use%20Nextcloud.pdf
/remote.php/dav/files/ruth/Readme.md
/remote.php/dav/files/ruth/Photos/
/remote.php/dav/files/ruth/Documents/
/remote.php/dav/files/ruth/Nextcloud%20intro.mp4
/remote.php/dav/files/ruth/pancake-recipe.txt
/remote.php/dav/files/ruth/book-club-notes.txt
(the PDFs, Photos/, Documents/ and Templates/ are the
skeleton files every new account starts with)

$ curl -u ruth:<redacted> $D/pancake-recipe.txt -o downloaded-recipe.txt
HTTP 200

$ sha256sum pancake-recipe-v1.txt downloaded-recipe.txt
15df1807ad27aa20776f431620ed9efe0a3de62999b2362bb3ef2fa6c843ed22  pancake-recipe-v1.txt

(byte-identical: what went up is what came back)
